Tinta — Support

Tinta is a reading tool for language learners on the Mac. Paste a text in the language you are learning, and every word is coloured by how common it is and whether you know it; click a word to see what it means.

Getting started

  1. Choose the language you are learning and the language you want explanations in. You can change both later in Settings.
  2. A short sample opens. Click a word to see its meaning. Press k to mark it known, l for learning, i to ignore it, u to reset it.
  3. To read your own text, open the text menu at the top left and choose New text (paste) or Open a text file… (a .txt file).

Press ? in the app for every keyboard shortcut.

Questions

A language is downloading, or will not load. Each language's dictionary is downloaded the first time you choose it, and needs an internet connection that once. After that it works offline. If it fails, check the connection and choose the language again.

My words are not there after moving from the direct download to the App Store version. The two versions keep their data separately, as macOS requires. Export your vocabulary from the old one (Stats → Export vocab…) and import it in the new one (Stats → Import vocab…).

How do I turn on the AI features? Settings → AI: choose a provider and paste an API key from that provider. The app asks your permission before it sends anything, and says what it will send. See the privacy policy for details.

Where is my data? On your Mac only. The app has no account and no server.

Read aloud is silent. It uses the voices installed on your Mac. Add one for the language in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices.
