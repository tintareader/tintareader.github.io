# Tinta — what the Spanish letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: end, start, between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
a||ah, as in father, short and clear
b||b, as in bed
b|between|a soft b: the lips almost touch but do not close
c||k, as in cat
c|beforefront|th, as in think, in Spain; s in Latin America
d||d, with the tongue against the teeth
d|between|soft, like the th in this
d|end|very soft, or silent: Madrid
e||e, as in bed, but tenser
f||f, as in fish
g||g, as in go
g|between|a soft g, the tongue not quite closing
g|beforefront|a breathy h from the throat, like Scottish loch
h||always silent
i||ee, as in see, short and clear
j||a breathy h from the throat, like Scottish loch
k||k; loanwords only
l||l, light and clear
m||m, as in man
n||n, as in no
ñ||ny, as in canyon
o||o, as in note, but pure, with no w at the end
p||p, without the puff of air of English p
q||only in qu, which says k
r||one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt of American butter
r|start|rolled: a trill of the tongue
s||s, as in sun, never z
t||t, with the tongue against the teeth
u||oo, as in food
v||the same as b: b, as in bed
v|between|a soft b: the lips almost touch but do not close
w||w; loanwords only
x||ks, as in taxi; h in some names, like México
y||y, as in yes; in many places closer to the j of jam
y|end|ee, a vowel, at the end of a word: hoy, muy
z||th, as in think, in Spain; s in Latin America
ch||ch, as in church
ll||y, as in yes; sh in Argentina
rr||rolled: a trill of the tongue
qu||k; the u is silent
á||a, with the stress written on it
é||e, with the stress written on it
í||i, with the stress written on it; día is dí-a
ó||o, with the stress written on it
ú||u, with the stress written on it
ü||a u that is said where it would be silent: pingüino
