# Tinta — what the Indonesian letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: end, start, between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
a||ah, as in father
b||b, as in bed
b|end|close to p at the end of a word
c||ch, as in church, never k or s
d||d, as in dog
d|end|close to t at the end of a word
e||usually a weak uh, as in about; in some words a full e, like the e in say but held steady, without gliding into an ee
f||f; mostly in loanwords, and often said as p
g||g, as in go, never j
h||h, said lightly
h|end|a soft breath at the end of a word, not silent
i||ee, as in see
j||j, as in jam
k||k, as in kite, but without a puff of air
k|end|a catch in the throat, as in uh-oh, at the end of a word
l||l, light and clear
m||m, as in man
n||n, as in no
o||o, as in note, but pure
p||p, without a puff of air
q||k; in Arabic loanwords only
r||rolled: a tap or trill of the tongue
s||s, as in see, never z
t||t, without a puff of air
u||oo, as in food
v||f; in loanwords
w||w, as in wet
x||ks; in loanwords only
y||y, as in yes
z||z, as in zoo; in loanwords
ng||ng, as in singer, with no g after it
ny||ny, as in canyon
sy||sh, as in ship
kh||ch, as in Scottish loch; in loanwords only
