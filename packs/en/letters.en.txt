# Tinta — what the English letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: end, start, between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
a||short a, as in cat; ay, as in make, when an e ends the word
b||b, as in bed
c||k, as in cat
c|beforefront|s, as in city
d||d, as in dog
e||short e, as in bed; ee in be and me
e|end|usually silent, and makes the vowel before it say its name: make, home
f||f, as in fish
g||g, as in go
g|beforefront|often j, as in gem, but not in get or give
h||h, as in hat
i||short i, as in sit; eye, as in time
j||j, as in jam
k||k, as in kite; silent before n: know
l||l, as in love
m||m, as in man
n||n, as in no
o||short o, as in hot; oh, as in home
p||p, as in pen
q||only in qu
r||r, with the tongue curled back
r|end|silent after a vowel in British English: car; said in American English
s||s, as in sun; often z, as in is
s|end|s or z: cats, dogs
t||t, as in top
u||short u, as in cut; oo, as in rule; you, as in use
v||v, as in van
w||w, as in wet; silent before r: write
x||ks, as in box
y||y, as in yes
y|end|a vowel at the end: ee in happy, eye in my
z||z, as in zoo
th||two sounds: soft in think, hard in this
sh||sh, as in ship
ch||ch, as in church; k in school, sh in chef
ph||f, as in phone
wh||w, as in what; h in who
ck||k; the vowel before it is short
ng||ng, as in sing
qu||kw, as in quick
ee||ee, as in see
oo||oo, as in food; short, as in book
