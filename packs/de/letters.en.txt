# Tinta — what the German letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: sun, article (Arabic's al-), end, start,
#            lastsyllable (the last vowel of several), between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
a||ah, as in father; shorter before two consonants
b||b, as in bed
b|end|p at the end of a word: ab says "ap"
c||rare outside ch and ck: k in most loanwords
c|beforefront|ts, as in cats
d||d, as in dog
d|end|t at the end of a word: Hund says "hunt"
e||ay, as in say, when long; e, as in bed, when short
e|end|a weak uh at the end of a word, as in sofa
e|lastsyllable|a weak uh in the last syllable: gehen says "GAY-uhn", Tage says "TAH-guh"
f||f, as in fish
g||g, as in go, never j
g|end|k at the end of a word: Tag says "tahk"
g|end+after:i|ich sound, not k, in the common -ig ending: König, zwanzig, wichtig
h||h, as in hat
h|aftervowel|usually silent after a vowel, which it makes long
i||ee, as in see, when long; i, as in sit, when short
j||y, as in yes
k||k, as in kite
l||l, as in leaf: light, never dark
m||m, as in man
n||n, as in no
o||oh, as in so but pure; short o, as in British pot
p||p, as in pen
q||only in qu, which says kv
r||made at the back of the throat, close to a gargle
r|end|close to a short uh at the end: Bier says "bee-uh"
s||s, as in sun
s|beforevowel|z, as in zoo, before a vowel
s|after:ch|s, as in sun, after ch: wachsen, Achse
t||t, as in top
u||oo, as in moon, when long; u, as in put, when short
v||f, as in fish; v in some loanwords
w||v, as in van
x||ks, as in box
y||like ü: say ee with your lips rounded
z||ts, as in cats, even at the start of a word
ä||e, as in bed; when long, like the vowel of air
ö||say ay with your lips rounded as for oh
ü||say ee with your lips rounded as for oo
ß||ss, as in kiss, after a long vowel
sch||sh, as in ship
ch||after a, o, u: as in Scottish loch; elsewhere a soft hiss, like the h of huge
ch|before:s|before s, usually k: sechs, Fuchs, wachsen; but the ch of Bach or ich where the s starts an ending: machst, nächste
ck||k, as in back; the vowel before it is short
ei||eye, as in my
ie||ee, as in see; in a few words from Latin, i then a weak e: Familie, Linie, Ferien
au||ow, as in how
eu||oy, as in boy; in a few borrowed words, e then u: Museum
äu||oy, as in boy
st||sht, at the start of a word
sp||shp, at the start of a word
