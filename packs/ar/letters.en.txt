# Tinta — what the Arabic letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: sun, article (Arabic's al-), end, start,
#            lastsyllable (the last vowel of several), between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
ا||long a, as in father; at the start of a word it carries a short vowel
ا|start|at the start of a word: a seat for a short a, i or u, not a long ā
ا|article|the a of al-, "the": said at the start of a sentence, dropped after a vowel (fi l-bayt)
ا|end|long ā at the end of a word (hunā); after a noun's last letter it often spells the ending -an instead (kitāban, "a book")
ب||b, as in bed
ت||t, as in stop: without the puff of air top has
ث||th, as in think
ج||j, as in jam; g, as in go, in Egypt
ح||a strong breathy h from deep in the throat, as when fogging a glass
خ||ch, as in Scottish loch
د||d, as in dog
ذ||th, as in this
ر||a rolled r: a tap or trill of the tongue
ز||z, as in zoo
س||s, as in sun
ش||sh, as in ship
ص||a heavy s: s with the back of the tongue raised
ض||a heavy d: d with the back of the tongue raised
ط||a heavy t: t with the back of the tongue raised
ظ||a heavy th, as in this
ع||a tight squeeze deep in the throat; English has no such sound
غ||a gargled sound, like a French r
ف||f, as in fish
ق||k said far back, at the throat
ك||k, as in skin: without the puff of air kite has
ل||l, as in lamp
ل|sun|the l of al-, "the", which takes the sound of the next letter: ash-shams, not al-shams
ل|article|the l of al-, "the": al-kitāb
م||m, as in moon
ن||n, as in noon
ه||h, as in hat, said even at the end of a word
و||w, as in wet; or a long oo, as in food
ي||y, as in yes; or a long ee, as in see
ء||a catch in the breath, as in the middle of uh-oh
أ||a catch in the breath, then a or u
إ||a catch in the breath, then i
آ||a catch in the breath, then a long a
ؤ||a catch in the breath, written on a wāw
ئ||a catch in the breath, written on a yāʾ
ة||a at the end of a word; t when an ending follows
ى||long a, as in father; only at the end of a word
َ||a short a, as in cat, on the letter it sits over
ُ||a short u, as in put, on the letter it sits over
ِ||a short i, as in sit, on the letter it sits under
ْ||no vowel: the letter it sits over ends its syllable
ّ||the letter under it is said twice, held a moment longer
ً||-an at the end of a word (shukran); usually written with an alif after it
ٌ||-un at the end of a word: the case ending of a noun that is not definite
ٍ||-in at the end of a word: the same ending after a preposition
