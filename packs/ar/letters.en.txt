# Tinta — what the Arabic letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: sun, article (Arabic's al-), end, start,
#            lastsyllable (the last vowel of several), between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
ا||long a, held: between the a of bad and the a of father; deeper, as in father, next to a heavy letter, q or r; at the start of a word it carries a short vowel
ا|start|at the start of a word: a seat for a short a, i or u, not a long ā
ا|article|the a of al-, "the": said at the start of a sentence, dropped after a vowel (fi l-bayt)
ا|end|long ā at the end of a word (hunā); after a noun's last letter it often spells the ending -an instead (kitāban, "a book")
ب||b, as in bed
ت||t, as in top, with the tongue against the back of the upper teeth
ث||th, as in think
ج||j, as in jam; g, as in go, in Egypt; the s of measure in Lebanon and Syria
ح||a hoarse, whispered h, pushed through a throat squeezed tight; not the soft h of hat
خ||ch, as in Scottish loch or German Bach
د||d, as in dog
ذ||th, as in this
ر||a single tap of the tongue, like the tt of American butter; rolled when doubled
ز||z, as in zoo
س||s, as in sun
ش||sh, as in ship
ص||a heavy s: s with the back of the tongue raised; the vowels beside it go deep, so a sounds as in father, not cat
ض||a heavy d: d with the back of the tongue raised; the vowels beside it go deep, so a sounds as in father, not cat
ط||a heavy t, with no puff of air: t with the back of the tongue raised; the vowels beside it go deep, as in father
ظ||a heavy th, as in this: the back of the tongue raised, the vowels beside it deep; many say it as a heavy z
ع||the voiced partner of ح: the throat squeezed tight while the voice sounds, like a strangled ah; English has no such sound
غ||a gargled sound, kh with the voice on, much like a French r
ف||f, as in fish
ق||k said much further back, the tongue against the uvula (the soft flap at the back of the mouth), not in the throat; the vowels beside it go deep
ك||k, as in kite
ل||l, as in lamp
ل|sun|the l of al-, "the", which takes the sound of the next letter: ash-shams, not al-shams
ل|article|the l of al-, "the": al-kitāb
م||m, as in moon
ن||n, as in noon
ه||h, as in hat, said even at the end of a word
و||w, as in wet; or a long oo, as in food
ي||y, as in yes; or a long ee, as in see
ء||a catch in the breath, as in the middle of uh-oh
أ||a catch in the breath, then a or u
إ||a catch in the breath, then i
آ||a catch in the breath, then a long a
ؤ||a catch in the breath, written on a wāw
ئ||a catch in the breath, written on a yāʾ
ة||-a at the end of a word (madrasa, "school"); -at when an ending or another noun follows (madrasatī, "my school")
ى||a long a at the end of a word, as in father: ي without its dots (ʻalā, "on")
َ||a short a, as in cat, on the letter it sits over; nearer the u of but beside a heavy letter, q or r
ُ||a short u, as in put, on the letter it sits over
ِ||a short i, as in sit, on the letter it sits under
ْ||no vowel: the letter it sits over ends its syllable
ّ||the letter under it is doubled: held a moment longer, as the k in bookcase (ḥubb, "love")
ً||-an at the end of a word (shukran); usually written with an alif after it
ٌ||-un at the end of a word: the ending of a noun that is not definite (kitābun, "a book")
ٍ||-in at the end of a word: the same ending after a preposition
# A tile's label for the two marks that have no sound of their own
ْ|tile|no vowel
