# Tinta — what the Arabic letters sound like, for a reader of Indonesian.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: end, start, between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to Indonesian words.
ا||a panjang, seperti a dalam bapak tetapi lebih lama; di awal kata, vokal pendek
ا|start|di awal kata, tempat vokal pendek (a, i, atau u), bukan ā panjang
ا|article|a dari al-, kata sandang: dibaca di awal kalimat, hilang setelah vokal (fi l-bayt)
ا|end|ā panjang di akhir kata (hunā); setelah huruf terakhir kata benda sering menulis akhiran -an (kitāban, "sebuah buku")
