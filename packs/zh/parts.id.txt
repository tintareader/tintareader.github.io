# Tinta — parts.txt's two notes about the Mandarin pack's characters, in Indonesian:
# how the parts make a character, and a part's own definition.
# char|hint|definition
#   hint           how the parts make the character, translated; only where
#                  the character is not a sound part plus a meaning part
#   definition     parts.txt's definition, translated, with as many
#                  ;-separated senses
# A field left empty is not translated, and the card leaves it out.
#
# Derived from Make Me a Hanzi, dictionary.txt, commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92
# — https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi — by Shaunak Kishore and its
# contributors, itself derived from Unihan (https://www.unicode.org/charts/unihan.html) and
# CJKlib (https://github.com/cburgmer/cjklib).

# Modified by Tinta on 2026-10-01: the hint and definition of parts.txt (see its own
