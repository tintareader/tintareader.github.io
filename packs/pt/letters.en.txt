# Tinta — what the Portuguese letters sound like, for a reader of English.
# This project's own work: unencumbered.
# key|context|note
#   context  empty for the letter in general; otherwise where in the word the
#            note applies: end, start, between (two vowels), beforefront (before
#            e, i and the like), beforevowel, aftervowel. The engine works out
#            where a letter stands; the first context with a note wins.
# Written for this reader, not translated: comparisons are to English words.
a||ah, as in father
a|end|a weak uh at the end of a word, as in sofa
b||b, as in bed
c||k, as in cat
c|beforefront|s, as in see
d||d, as in dog
d|before:i|j, as in jeans, before i in Brazil: dia
d|before:e+penult|j, as in jeans, before a final weak e in Brazil: cidade
e||ay, as in say
e|end|ee, as in see, at the end of a word in Brazil: leite
f||f, as in fish
g||g, as in go
g|beforefront|zh, like the s of measure
h||always silent
i||ee, as in see
j||zh, like the s of measure
k||k; loanwords only
l||l, light and clear
l|end|w at the end of a syllable in Brazil: Brasil says "brazeew"
l|beforeconsonant|also w before a consonant in Brazil: alto says "owtoo"
m||m, as in man
m|end|not said as m: it makes the vowel before it nasal
m|beforeconsonant|not said as m before a consonant: it makes the vowel before it nasal, as in campo
n||n, as in no
n|beforeconsonant|not said as n before a consonant: it makes the vowel before it nasal, as in mundo
o||oh, as in so
o|end|oo, as in food, at the end of a word: livro
p||p, as in pen
q||only in qu
r||one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt of American butter
r|start|like an English h, at the start of a word in Brazil: rio
r|end|a light h, or a tap, at the end of a syllable
s||s, as in see
s|between|z, as in zoo, between two vowels
s|end|s; sh in Rio and in Portugal, already voiced to z before a voiced consonant: mesmo
t||t, as in top
t|before:i|ch, as in cheese, before i in Brazil: tia
t|before:e+penult|ch, as in cheese, before a final weak e in Brazil: leite
u||oo, as in food
v||v, as in van
w||w; loanwords only
x||sh, as in ship, most often; also ks, z or s
y||ee; loanwords only
z||z, as in zoo
z|end|s at the end of a word: feliz
lh||ly, close to the lli of million
nh||ny, as in canyon
ch||sh, as in ship
rr||like an English h, from the throat: carro
ss||s, as in see; written ss between vowels, where one s says z
qu||k before e and i, though kw in some words (cinquenta, frequente); kw before a and o
ão||a nasal ow: the ow of how said through the nose
õe||a nasal oy: the oy of boy said through the nose
ãe||a nasal eye: the word eye said through the nose
á||ah, with the stress written on it
à||a; it is a + a run together
â||a closed uh, with the stress on it
ã||a nasal uh: the u of cut said through the nose
é||open e, as in bed, with the stress on it
ê||closed e, as in say, with the stress on it
í||ee, with the stress written on it
ó||open o, as in British pot, with the stress on it
ô||closed o, as in so, with the stress on it
õ||a nasal o: o said through the nose
ú||oo, with the stress written on it
ç||s, as in see
